# 社区行为准则 / Code of conduct

欢迎不同背景、经验与观点的贡献者。请尊重他人，围绕问题与证据讨论，耐心对待初学者，并妥善保护私人信息。

不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击、公开他人隐私或垃圾信息。本准则适用于项目 Issues、Pull Requests 及其他项目管理的交流空间。

维护者可要求修改内容、关闭讨论或限制反复违规者参与。若发现问题，可在相关讨论中联系维护者；涉及隐私时先请求私下沟通，不重复传播敏感内容。处理时应考虑上下文，并允许当事人解释。

We welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Be respectful, discuss ideas with evidence, help newcomers, and protect private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and spam are not acceptable in project-managed spaces. Maintainers may request edits, close discussions, or restrict repeated violations. Contact a maintainer to report concerns; request a private channel for sensitive matters. Reports should be handled with context and an opportunity to respond.
